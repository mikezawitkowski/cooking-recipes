# Peas Merry and Bright

Prep: XX min
Cook: XX min
Ready In XX min


## Ingredients


### Wet

* green peas, frozen, shelled
* one lemon for juice and zest
* one fennel
* butter, unsalted


## Instructions

* boil peas in salted water until done, per the package, and drain and rinse for 5 seconds in cold water to stop the cooking and remove excess salt
* in a skillet big enough to hold the peas add a generous amount of unsalted butter until melted
* add fennel chopped into bite sized pieces (cut off the end and the stalks, just the main body) into the butter to heat through. The longer you cook it the less crunchy and less anise flavor you get but the sweeter it becomes.
* then add the peas to the mix
* zest a lemon over the mixture
* taste and adjust brightness by mixing lemon juice into the butter
* add salt and pepper to taste
