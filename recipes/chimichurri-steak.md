# Steak with Chimichurri Sauce

Starting with a recipe for "Chimichurri Sauce" from loveandlemons.com I evolved this recipe until my partner insisted this become part of our weekly meal rotation.

## Ingredients

* flank steak, between 1/2 lb to 1 lb
* 1/3 c olive oil
* 2 T rice wine vinegar (was supposed to be white wine originally but I subbed it out)
* 1 garlic clove minced
* 1/2 tsp sea salt for the sauce, plus a liberal amount of salt to cover the steak
* 1/4 tsp oregano
* 1/4 tsp crushed red pepper flakes
* 1/2 c finely chopped fresh flat leaf parsley w/ coarse stems removed
* 1 T avocado oil or some other high smoke point oil

### Other optional ingredients

* Fresh corn on the cob, husked
* yams or sweet potatoes, one per person


## Instructions

* Take the thawed flank steak and season every surface throwing salt at it, and then leave out at room temperature on a plate to absorb the salt while you work on the rest of the ingredients.
* If you are cooking a starchy root in the microwave, start doing that. If you have corn to boil, start the water.
* Mix together all of the above sauce ingredients into a small bowl using a whisk, in order of appearance above. Set aside the bowl.
* In a cast iron skillet on medium, add a 1 T avocado oil and once hot add the steak getting it to the internal temperature you prefer (I did medium well). Then let it rest on a cutting board for at least 1/3 of the time that it cooked on the stove for, but no shorter than 5 minutes. 
* Once rested so juices won't abandon the meat, slice into strips and you can optionally saute the strips again to get it fully cooked through.
* Add to the plate along with the other cooked ingredients and as a final touch drizzle the chimichurri sauce over the steak slices.
 

 
## Tips, Tricks, Variations

* I made this on the 21st of May 2026 with just 1/2 lb of flank steak instead of a whole one. I salted the raw steak and left it out at room temperature while I prepared the sauce. In parallel, I microwaved yams stabbed with a fork on both sides and microwaved on high 5 minutes each side, while simultaneously boiled water for corn using 1 T of sugar and 1 T of salt in the water and cooked the corn for about 5 minutes.
