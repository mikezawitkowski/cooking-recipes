# Orange Olive Oil Cake

Prep: XX min
Cook: XX min
Ready In XX min


## Ingredients


### Wet

* 8 oranges for zest, and about 3 oranges squeezed to create 1/2 c of fresh orange juice
* 3 eggs
* 3/4 c extra virgin olive oil
* 1 1/3 c white sugar
* 1 tsp vanilla extract
* 3 T orange zest + an additional 1 tsp orange zest in place of orange extract

### Dry

* 2 c gf flour
* 1/2 c blanched almond flour
* 1/2 tsp baking soda
* 1/2 tsp baking powder
* 1/2 tsp salt

### Misc ingredients

* just enough butter to coat the inside of your bundt cake pan, and just enough flour to sprinkle on that butter
* (optional) ingredients to make a cranberry glaze, which I normally omit so I won't include here. I tried it once but instead of giving it a nice tang it was waaay too sweet so I'll omit it for now.


## Directions

* Preheat oven to 350 degrees F
* Grease and line pan, and/or use butter to coat the bundt pan, plus flour to sprinkle over the butter
* In medium mixing bowl add all wet ingredients together including all zest ingredients and orange juice
* Beat w/ an electric mixer until well combined, about 30 seconds
* Add dry ingredeints making sure to spoon and level the flour
* Use a blender to mix all together but careful not to overbeat anything, just ["fold it"](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=fCVKCUB5w50) together.
* transfer to prepared pan
* bake in center of oven for 45-53 minutes or until toothpick comes out clean with a few moist crumbs
* let cool for about 10 min then release (flip) from pan, then let cool completely
* (optional) serve with slices of oranges and/or whipped cream and/or cranberry glaze drizzled on the bundt cake

## Key Notes and Tips and Tricks

* It took me about 7 oranges to get enough orange zest. In fact, I started to compact it in order to better measure, so more like 2 T were added plus the 1 tsp at the beginning. According to those who tested it afterwards, there was sufficient zest, but also I was told "you can never have enough zest". So that's why we have 8 oranges for zest to be safe, and no need to buy orange juice because we have plenty of it here.
